Connected social accounts

Connect a social account once and the Engine's browser agent stays logged in for every lead — no per-lead login. On your own computer, run this one command (installs nothing permanent), log in normally in the window that opens, then close it:
npx playwright@latest codegen --save-storage=session.json https://www.instagram.com
Swap the URL for the platform you're connecting. Then open the generated session.json, copy everything, and paste it below. It's your live login — treat it like a password; it's stored encrypted.
